TetravanRx

Hydroxyethyl Starch (6%) 130/0.4 in Isotonic Sodium Chloride Intravenous Infusion

A third-generation colloid infusion, positioned as a safe alternative to blood when actual blood is not immediately available, for loss of blood up to 1 litre.

Product reference — Tetravan

Indications

  • Treatment of hypovolemia
  • Treatment of shock
  • Emergency situations when actual blood is not available
  • Fluid resuscitation
  • Severe hypoalbuminemia or disease of large protein loss, e.g. burns

Dosage & administration

Positioning

  • 3rd generation, among the safest colloids available
  • Less impact on renal function
  • Lower chance of anaphylactic reactions
  • Preserves coagulation system
  • Minimal chance of pruritus

Advantages over conventional expanders

  • Over polygeline & gelatin: fewer allergic reactions, less impact on renal function
  • Over albumin: no risk of blood-borne disease, better cardiac performance unlike cardiac oedema
  • Over Ringer's lactate: less oedema, lower lower dilution of clotting factors
Positioning: Effective and safe, no risk of contamination, replaces lost blood on a 70% basis — a safe alternative to blood for losses of up to 1 litre. Available as 6% w/v, 500 ml Eurohead plastic bottle.

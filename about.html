What we do

Medisky markets a compact, guideline-aligned range of hospital and pharmacy products across two areas: critical-care nutrition & injectable therapeutics — amino acid infusions, glutamine therapy, trace-element and iron formulations, and plasma volume expanders — and surgical consumables, currently nitrile examination gloves.

Rather than carrying a broad, unfocused catalogue, we've built our portfolio around therapy areas that come up constantly on the ICU and surgical floor: nutritional support for the critically ill, correction of anaemia and trace-element deficiency, and fluid resuscitation when blood products aren't immediately available. Every formulation in the range is referenced against ESPEN (European Society for Clinical Nutrition and Metabolism) dosing standards, so clinicians can adopt them into existing protocols without friction.

We work directly with hospitals, nursing homes, institutional buyers and retail pharmacy stockists across Bihar and neighbouring states, with room to expand our distributor network further into Eastern India.

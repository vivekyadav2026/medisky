GlutaskyRx

L-alanyl-L-glutamine infusion 20% w/v

A synthetic glutamine dipeptide infusion, administered alone or alongside total parenteral nutrition, to enhance recovery and optimise nutrition in critically ill patients.

Product reference — Glutasky

Indications

  • ICU patients
  • Gastrointestinal surgery patients
  • Pancreatitis patients
  • Severe burn patients
  • Cancer chemotherapy patients
  • Acute and chronic infection
  • Multiple organ dysfunction syndrome
  • Immunodeficiency syndrome, including AIDS

Dosage & administration

Dosage

  • 1.5–2.5 ml of Glutasky IV per kg body weight (equivalent to 0.3–0.5 g L-alanyl-L-glutamine/kg body weight)
  • Equates to 100–175 ml for a patient of 70 kg body weight
  • Maximum daily dose: 2.5 ml/kg, equivalent to 0.5 g L-alanyl-L-glutamine/kg body weight

Administration

  • For peripheral venous infusion, dilute to an osmolarity below 800 mosmol/L (e.g. 50 ml Glutasky IV + 50 ml NaCl)
  • Infusion rate: 0.5 ml/minute
Clinical benefit: Boosts immune system and improves cell metabolism, reduces oxidative stress, preserves metabolic function, and demonstrates immunomodulatory and GI mucosal protective activity.

Aminosky-10%Rx

Amino Acid (8 essential & 10 non-essential AA) 10% w/v with electrolytes

A balanced amino-acid infusion formulated to maintain protein, energy, fluid and electrolyte balance, as per ESPEN standards.

Product reference — Aminosky-10%

Indications

  • Infectious conditions
  • Prolonged illness
  • Recovery from weakness
  • Improved nitrogen balance
  • Fatigue due to undiagnosed causes
  • Burn patients
  • Anorexia

Dosage & administration

Dosage

  • Recommended dosage: 1–2 g amino acids / kg body weight / day
  • Equivalent to 1–2 bottles per day for 5 days

Administration

  • Available as 250 ml single-dose bottle, for IV use only
  • Infusion rate: up to 2 ml/minute
  • Drop rate: 35–50 drops/minute
Note: Maintains protein, energy, fluid and electrolyte balance in patients with infectious conditions, prolonged illness or burns, formulated as per ESPEN standards.

Ferric-CMRx

Ferric Carboxymaltose Injection, 500 mg / 10 ml (50 mg/ml elemental iron)

A third-generation parenteral iron formulation for iron deficiency anaemia, positioned on safety, efficacy and speed of correction.

Product reference — Ferric-CM

Indications

  • Iron deficiency anaemia (IDA)
  • Patients with non-dialysis dependent CKD
  • Unsatisfactory response to oral iron
  • Pre & post-operative anaemia
  • IDA due to abnormal uterine bleeding

Dosage & administration

Dosage

  • Injectable solution 50 mg/ml, available in 2 ml, 15 ml & 20 ml single-dose vials; each ml contains 50 mg elemental iron
  • ≥50 kg body weight: 750 mg IV in 2 doses separated by at least 7 days, not exceeding a cumulative dose of 1500 mg per course
  • Alternatively, may be administered as 15 mg/kg IV as a single dose, not exceeding 1000 mg
  • ≤50 kg body weight: 15 mg/kg IV in 2 doses separated by at least 7 days

Dilution for IV infusion

  • 2–4 ml (100–200 mg iron): dilute in max 50 ml 0.9% NaCl, administer over 3 minutes
  • 4–10 ml (200–500 mg iron): dilute in max 100 ml 0.9% NaCl, administer over 6 minutes
  • 10–20 ml (500–1000 mg iron): dilute in max 250 ml 0.9% NaCl, administer over 15 minutes
Clinical data: Ferric-CM has been shown to significantly increase haemoglobin by 4.4 g/dL at 6 weeks. Must be diluted only in sterile 0.9% w/v NaCl solution when given as an infusion.

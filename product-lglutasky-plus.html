L-Glutasky PlusRx

L-glutamine 10 g + beta-carotene 1.5 mg + vitamin C 13 mg + vitamin E 2.83 mg + zinc 3.4 mg + selenium 0.0113 mg, sachet

An oral glutamine and antioxidant sachet for critically ill patients, built around the idea of a stronger gut for stronger immunity.

Product reference — L-Glutasky Plus

Indications

  • Patients with oral mucositis due to trauma or disease
  • GI surgery — improves gut function, reduces perioperative complications
  • ICU / critically ill — reduces nosocomial infection and hospital stay, maintains gut integrity
  • Pre & post surgery — reduces inflammatory response and post-surgery risk
  • Burn patients — supports better wound healing

Dosage & administration

Composition per sachet

  • L-glutamine 10 g
  • Beta-carotene 1.5 mg, Vitamin C 13 mg, Vitamin E 2.83 mg
  • Zinc 3.4 mg, Selenium 0.0113 mg

Mechanism

  • Inhibits apoptosis and promotes rapid proliferation of mucosal cells
  • Repairs gastrointestinal mucosa
  • Alleviates treatment-related toxicities
Clinical evidence: Patients with oral mucositis due to trauma or disease healed faster when nutritional support with glutamine (GLN) was provided.
